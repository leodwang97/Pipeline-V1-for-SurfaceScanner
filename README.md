Field Survey and Dataset Availability
To evaluate and validate the proposed pipeline, a field survey was conducted in Thailand in 2024. 
The collected data were subsequently processed and analyzed using the developed pipeline to assess its applicability and performance under real-world field conditions.

The associated datasets are available upon reasonable request for research purposes. Access can be requested through the following link:
